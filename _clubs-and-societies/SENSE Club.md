<p><strong>S.EN.S.E Club</strong> is a merger of our Eco Club and Science
Club and the acronym <strong>S.E.N.S.E</strong> represents: Science, Environment,
Sustainability, and Eco-Friendly.
<br>
<br>S.EN.S.E Club aims to increase Kemingnites’ awareness of&nbsp;current
environmental issues through&nbsp;hands-on experiences and meaningful engagement.
Through these experiences, students become Green Ambassadors who take purposeful
actions to support sustainable living in their everyday lives.</p>
